A post is simply a Markdown file in `_posts/` whose filename begins with a date:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-title-of-the-post.md
```

Use ordinary Markdown. Raw HTML also works when you need it.

## A small claim

The site should feel like a document first and a designed object second. That
means readable text, obvious links, durable URLs, and almost no ornamental UI.

> The visual personality should come mostly from the writing and organization.
